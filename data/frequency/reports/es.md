## Sources

- **Frequency:** word counts from OpenSubtitles 2018 (hermitdave/FrequencyWords, CC BY-SA 4.0) merged with the Real Academia Española CREA frequency list, as published in doozan/spanish_data (es_merged_50k.txt). CREA covers written and spoken Spanish from Spain and Latin America. No Colombia-only frequency list is openly available.
- **Lemmatization:** English Wiktionary inflection data (doozan/spanish_data es_allforms.csv, CC BY-SA)
- **Disambiguation:** UD Spanish AnCora treebank (CC BY 4.0), used only to split ambiguous forms such as la (article or pronoun)
- **Definitions and parts of speech:** English Wiktionary as extracted by doozan/spanish_data (es-en.data, November 2026; CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL)
- **Definitions where Wiktionary data has no entry:** English Wiktionary, via the Wiktextract/Kaikki.org extraction in Vuizur/Wiktionary-Dictionaries (2022–23 snapshot; CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL), for the few words the November 2026 extraction lacks (such as the preposition a)

## Meaning review

## Sentences on left-out topics

Sentences about Africa, India, the Middle East, Islam or the Bible are left out of this app. 175 were replaced by AI-written sentences set in Europe, Asia or North America that use the word the same way, 41 were dropped (the word itself is on these topics, or no replacement was written yet), and 1 more matched a keyword without being about these topics.

## Crude sentences

Crude sentences (swearing, sexual content, slurs and vulgar insults, crude toilet talk) are left out of this app. 121 were replaced by AI-written sentences that use the word in a clean meaning, 70 were dropped (the word itself is crude, or no replacement was written yet), and 90 more matched a keyword without being crude.

## Sentences on politics, war or living people

Sentences on politics, war or violence, or that name living people, are left out of this app (and, in the Ukrainian and Russian sets, sentences on the other country). 878 were replaced by AI-written sentences that use the word the same way, 42 were dropped, and 288 more matched a keyword without being on these subjects.

## Crude meanings

82 meanings were dropped and 22 glosses reworded, to keep out crude slang, offensive English and political slang and to correct a few wrong meanings and place names, in 80 words.

## Words left out as crude
