## Meaning review

## Sentences on left-out topics

Sentences about Africa, India, the Middle East, Islam or the Bible are left out of this app. 145 were replaced by AI-written sentences set in Europe, Asia or North America that use the word the same way, 68 were dropped (the word itself is on these topics, or no replacement was written yet), and 32 more matched a keyword without being about these topics.

## Crude sentences

Crude sentences (swearing, sexual content, slurs and vulgar insults, crude toilet talk) are left out of this app. 73 were replaced by AI-written sentences that use the word in a clean meaning, 27 were dropped (the word itself is crude, or no replacement was written yet), and 32 more matched a keyword without being crude.

## Sentences on politics, war or living people

Sentences on politics, war or violence, or that name living people, are left out of this app (and, in the Ukrainian and Russian sets, sentences on the other country). 842 were replaced by AI-written sentences that use the word the same way, 77 were dropped, and 261 more matched a keyword without being on these subjects.

## Crude meanings

36 meanings were dropped and 12 glosses reworded, to keep out crude slang, offensive English and political slang and to correct a few wrong meanings and place names, in 39 words.

## Words left out as crude

9 words that are vulgar, slurs, curses or words for sex in every meaning are left out of this set: секс, блядь, дерьмо, шлюха, жопа, проститутка, изнасилование, девка, хрень.

## Sentences for words left without any

41 words that had no example sentence got 82 AI-written ones: calm, general sentences for words about subjects the app otherwise leaves out, and neutral facts for words that name such a subject.
